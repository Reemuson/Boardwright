### Deferred findings (recorded at 0.1.4 E2E approval, 2026-11-18)

The boardwright-ci-test 0.1.4 E2E release was approved as a draft test release
only. These findings are open and are not covered by that approval; it is not a
production approval.

- **D1: schematic cover and revision history.** The schematic cover sheet still
  shows placeholder content, and the visible revision history is incomplete:
  releases before the ledger have no lettered rows.
- **D2: drawing PDFs are informational.** The accepted output manifest
  categorises the fabrication and assembly PDFs as `informational`
  (`output_manifest.py`). Baseline comparison therefore does not control them,
  which is how the 0.1.3 fabrication drawing's wrong feature values (F17) passed
  unnoticed. Text extraction cannot close this: the notes and tables are drawn
  in KiCad stroke font, and `pdftotext` finds no feature-table values. The
  interim control used for 0.1.4/0.1.5 is
  `boardwright-e2e/compare_drawing_pdfs.py`. It renders each page at 200 dpi,
  masks only the title block, and reports any other changed region. Two CI builds
  of the same source rendered pixel-identical, and the comparison detects the
  0.1.3 -> 0.1.4 feature-table correction. It runs outside CI and its
  title-block mask is specific to the Boardwright A4 worksheets, so drawing
  content remains a **production-readiness limitation**.
- **D3: DRC clearance violation.** The 0.1917 mm clearance deviation (F15)
  remains the single DRC error on FIC-100A; it is documented, not waived.
